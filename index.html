Our Philosophy

Four Core Goals of GYAC

  • 01

    Teach Fundamentals

    Coaches teach the core athletic fundamentals of Olympic-style boxing in a safe, structured ring environment.

  • 02

    Athletic Diversion

    Provide a powerful, healthy physical diversion to negative outside influences like drug use, gangs, and violence.

  • 03

    Character Growth

    Instill and focus on critical life values: self-esteem, active goal-setting, discipline, mutual respect, and personal responsibility.

  • 04

    Handling Life's Ring

    Equip young athletes with the mental toughness to handle triumphs and challenges outside of the boxing ring.

In Memoriam

Remembering Carlos Duncan

"Adults called him by his first name. The kids knew him as Mr. D."

Carlos spent his entire life dedicated to youth development. The Galesburg Youth Athletic Club was originally founded by his father, Howard "Andy" Duncan, and later managed by his brother before Carlos took leadership in the early 1990s.

An elite veteran, Mr. Duncan served in the U.S. Air Force and rose to become chairman for the U.S. Illinois Boxing Association Coaches Committee. He was recognized as a Certified Level IV elite coach, clinician, and official with U.S. Amateur Boxing Inc.

Beyond the gym, Carlos served on boards for Knox County United Way, Knox County Teen Court, Knox County YMCA, Knox County Healthy Youth Alliance, and the Galesburg Youth Commission. He worked in Galesburg School District 205 and mentored students at the Literacy Coalition of Carl Sandburg College.

Carlos passed away on July 2, 2022, at the age of 80. His legacy of respect, discipline, and absolute belief in the youth of Galesburg continues to drive every single punch thrown inside GYAC today.

  • 1946
    Howard "Andy" Duncan establishes the gym to support youth athletic initiatives.
  • Early 1990s
    Carlos "Mr. D" Duncan takes over operations, expanding community outreach and certified USA Boxing coaching.
  • 2022
    Passing of Carlos Duncan. The gym's leadership is passed to dedicated disciples and veteran coaches.
  • Present Day
    GYAC continues as a 100% volunteer-led home of mentorship, academic discipline, and athletic triumph.
More Than Boxing

Our Gym's Story

GYAC provides a healthy, rigorous, and inclusive environment for boys and girls ages 8 to 18 to master the fundamentals of Olympic-style boxing and participate in competitive Junior Olympic matches.

Our open-door, volunteer-driven atmosphere quickly forms strong, lifelong bonds between coaches, parents, and young athletes. We pride ourselves on structured discipline that extends far beyond physical workouts.

As part of our commitment to Galesburg's youth, GYAC proudly partners with the **Knox County Teen Court** and the **Knox County Probation Office**, providing positive role models, second chances, and academic guidelines for youth who need active mentoring.

"I was in seventh grade when we moved here, and honestly, I had a hard time fitting in. I started going to the GYAC with a friend of mine. Now, I bring her kid with me and my kid. It's been 21 years I've been coming here and I've learned so much."

Julie Guiste

Coach & Mentor

"Carlos didn't write me off. I was 12 and plenty of folks did. But I got to come to a place where people saw me and accepted me. I learned how to stand tall, set goals, and carry myself with respect."

Schedule & Guidelines

Our Olympic-style boxing programs are structured to help young people excel physically, mentally, and academically. Review our gym's student guidelines below:

  • 1
    Be On Time Punctuality represents focus, dedication, and respect for your trainers and peers.

    Absolute Respect Zero tolerance for bullying. Display absolute respect to everyone inside and outside the ring.
  • 3
    Maintain a "C" Grade Average School comes first. Boxers failing to hit a C average will work with our tutors until grades improve.
